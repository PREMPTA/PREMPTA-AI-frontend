Stage 1 of 7

Sign up and set up the organization

Two screens capture who is using PREMPTA and the organization being analysed. Values PREMPTA already knows are filled in for you.

1

Create the account

Sign-up page
Alex Morganalex.morgan@watkins.example

One form with two short sections: about you, and your organization.

What to do

  1. Select Use demo details.
  2. Select Create account.

What to point out

  • Five fields: full name, work email and password, then organization name and legal business name.
  • Each missing or invalid field is flagged on its own. The password is never stored in this demo.
  • The stepper shows the whole journey: sign up, organization, connect data, agents, insights.
2

Confirm the organization profile

Setup › Organization profile
Alex Morganalex.morgan@watkins.example

The Organization Profile from the full application, reduced to what the analysis needs.

What to do

  1. Review the prefilled values.
  2. Select Save and continue.

What to point out

  • Every field shows where its value came from: From sign-up, From records or Client reference. Editing a field marks it Entered.
  • From the records: the address on the FieldFlow timesheet header, 47 employees and 17 active jobs from the QuickBooks export.
  • Your name and email from sign-up become the primary contact, so nothing is typed twice.
Stage 2 of 7

Connect FieldFlow and QuickBooks

Each system is connected once. The details entered here become the configuration of its integration agent.

3

Enter the connection details

Setup › Connect data
Alex Morganalex.morgan@watkins.example

FieldFlow and QuickBooks each ask for the same few details.

What to do

  1. Review the agent mailbox and the reports to collect.
  2. Select Fill demo values to add the credentials.

What to point out

  • The agent mailbox comes from your email domain: prempta.fieldflow@watkins.example and prempta.quickbooks@watkins.example.
  • FieldFlow collects daily timesheets and daily activity reports. QuickBooks collects the Time by Name Detail report for the company Watkins Environmental, Inc.
  • Without a credential, the connection is stopped with a clear message.
4

Connect and check what arrived

Connect data › Connected
Alex Morganalex.morgan@watkins.example

Once connected, each card summarises its connection and what it received.

What to do

  1. Select Connect FieldFlow, then Connect QuickBooks.
  2. Select Add case files.
  3. Select Received files to see the files.

What to point out

  • The credential is kept only as a vault reference, such as vault://watkins/fieldflow/…. The typed value is discarded.
  • FieldFlow delivered 11 shifts over 2 days and 1 activity report; QuickBooks delivered 242 pay lines for 47 employees.
  • Case files need no agent. Files that don’t belong in the analysis can’t be selected, and the complaint is listed as Class action complaint (anonymized).
5

Check the data is ready

  • Without payroll the bar shows Payroll needed and Set up agents stays disabled.
  • Nov 13 is in both sources. Nov 19 falls after the payroll export, which covers Nov 8–14.
Stage 3 of 7

Configure the integration agents

The agents are set up from the connection details. Nothing is entered twice.

6

Watch the agents configure themselves

Setup › Integration agents
Alex Morganalex.morgan@watkins.example

Each agent works through the setup checklist from the full application, using the details entered on Connect data.

What to do

  1. Wait a moment while both agents complete their checklist.
  2. Optionally change how often each mailbox is checked and when reports are processed.

What to point out

  • The checklist: dedicated mailbox, mailbox access delegated, reports routed, credential stored and a connection test. Each step shows the value it used.
  • Mailbox, username, sign-in method, credential reference and reports are all inherited. Only the check frequency and processing schedule can be changed here.
  • Setup, the connection test and the first sync are simulated and labelled as such.
