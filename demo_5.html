Set up the PAGA Notice Agent

Agents › PAGA Notice Agent
Alex Morganalex.morgan@watkins.example

A file agent. You give it the folder where PAGA notices are saved, and it reads every new document that lands there: dates, alleged violations, required actions and deadlines.

What to do

  1. Type the path of the shared folder in Notices folder, or select Fill demo folder.
  2. Check the case owner and the documents to accept.
  3. Select Save and check folder.

What you’re seeing

  • The folder box takes a network share, a drive path or a /path. Its example reads “\\fileserver\Legal\PAGA Notices”. Prempta only reads the folder; it never moves, changes or deletes files.
  • Without a folder the setup is 2 of 3 complete and the check is stopped: “Enter the folder where PAGA notices are saved.”
  • The demo folder is \\fs01.watkins.example\Legal\PAGA Notices. The check opens the folder, confirms read access and looks for new documents.
  • Once active, the panel shows what it watches (“Watching \\fs01.watkins.example\Legal\PAGA Notices, checked every 15 minutes”) and the document it found. New documents are reviewed straight away, and Check the folder now checks again on demand.
  • The sample cure plan is reviewed in three steps. It comes from another employer, so it is marked Not linked to Watkins Environmental and no deadlines are added.
  • From the document: PAGA notice filed July 16, 2025; cure conference September 29, 2025; cure actions due within 45 days of the conference; proof of completion due November 13, 2025.
  • It maps the plan’s asks to Prempta’s checks. For example, “premiums shown as their own line on the wage statement” maps to the finding “Break premium noted but not paid”.

Legal terms in plain English

PAGA
The Private Attorneys General Act (Labor Code sections 2698 to 2699.8). An employee who experienced Labor Code violations can seek civil penalties on behalf of the state and other affected employees.
LWDA
The California Labor and Workforce Development Agency. A PAGA claim starts with a written notice filed with LWDA and sent to the employer.
Cure plan
LWDA’s list of what an employer must do, and by when, to fix the alleged violations. This one followed a cure conference under Labor Code section 2699.3(c)(2)(B).
PAGA period
The year before the notice was filed.

How it helps you decideWhen a real notice arrives, the owner sees the deadlines and the evidence LWDA expects next to the checks that produce it.

Read the PAGA Monitoring Agent

Agents › PAGA Monitoring Agent
Alex Morganalex.morgan@watkins.example

A live agent with nothing to set up. It watches official California sources for PAGA changes and says what each one means for you.

What to do

  1. Select the PAGA Monitoring Agent.
  2. Open any source link. Each one opens the official page.

What you’re seeing

  • Status: Always on. There is no setup form.
  • Rulemaking: LWDA’s proposed PAGA regulations, OAL notice file Z2026-0121-03. The second comment period closed August 18, 2026, and no adoption or effective date is posted yet.
  • The 2024 reform, for notices filed on or after June 19, 2024: who can bring a claim, penalty caps of 15% or 30%, the small-employer cure within 33 days, early evaluation, and the 65/35 penalty split.
  • Filing: notices, responses and cure proposals go through the DIR PAGA filing portal, and a $75 fee applies.
  • Six sources, all on labor.ca.gov, dir.ca.gov and leginfo.legislature.ca.gov. The summaries were checked against those pages on Nov 23, 2026. The demo shows that checked snapshot; it doesn’t fetch live.

Legal terms in plain English

Penalty cap
The most PAGA penalties can be when the employer was taking all reasonable steps to comply: 15% if it was already doing so before the notice, 30% if it starts within 60 days after.
Small-employer cure
Employers with fewer than 100 employees in the year before a notice can send LWDA a confidential cure proposal within 33 days of receiving it.
Early evaluation conference
An early, court-run meeting an employer can ask for, filed with or before its response to the lawsuit.

How it helps you decideLegal changes reach the people who act on them, in plain words and tied to the organization’s own data. For example, the 47 employees in the payroll export are set against the 100-employee cure threshold.

Legal terms in plain English

Straight time and overtime
Straight time is the regular hourly rate. California overtime is 1.5 times that rate for hours over 8 in a workday or 40 in a workweek.
Premium pay
One extra hour of pay at the regular rate for a workday when a required meal or rest break wasn’t provided (Labor Code section 226.7).
Federal enclave
Federal land, such as a military base, where some California employment laws may not apply. Counsel decides whether it applies to a job.

How it helps you decideThe list turns 71 flags into four concrete next steps, ordered by urgency and money.

Each flagged record takes a decision and a note, and the whole set exports for counsel.

What to do

  1. In an open record, select Confirmed issue, Not an issue or Needs follow-up, and add a note if needed.
  2. Select Mark the 34 undecided as confirmed to settle a whole finding at once.

What you’re seeing
