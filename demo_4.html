How to read the results

HighPayroll shows pay or a premium that appears to be owed.
MediumThe records can’t show compliance, or match a practice the complaint alleges.
LowA record-keeping issue.
PassChecked and consistent across sources.
Demo estimateA dollar figure from demo pay rates, not client financials.
Client referenceA value from documents the client shared with Prempta, such as the use-case proposal.

Every count and finding comes from the supplied files. Results are potential exceptions for review, not legal conclusions.

Setup › Organization profile
Alex Morganalex.morgan@watkins.example

Eight fields, already filled in. Review them and save.

What to do

  2. Select Save and continue.

What to point out

Set up the PAGA Notice Agent

Agents › PAGA Notice Agent
Alex Morganalex.morgan@watkins.example

A dedicated mailbox for legal notices. Counsel, HR or the registered agent forward notices and complaints to it.

What to do

  1. Select Add case documents to upload the complaint you already have.
  2. Select Fill demo credential, then Save and test connection.

What to point out

  • Mailbox prempta.notices@watkins.example. The case owner, alex.morgan@watkins.example, is alerted when a case opens; counsel can be copied.
  • Available now, when a PDF arrives: read and extract it, identify the complaint and open a case, extract the claims, allegations and class definition, find deadlines the document states, and alert the case owner and counsel.
  • Planned, and clearly marked not active: watching the LWDA PAGA case search for new notices that name the client.
  • The test runs on the uploaded complaint: 9 causes of action, general allegations ¶24–47 of 86 paragraphs, and the class definition from ¶20(a). No deadline is stated in the document, and the screen says so instead of inventing one.
