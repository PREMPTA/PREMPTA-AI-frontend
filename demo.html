Sign in and onboard the organization

The Platform Administrator sets up Watkins Environmental on PREMPTA AI in a guided, six-step flow. Everything entered here drives the rest of the application.

  1. Under Demo sign-in, choose Alex Morgan — Platform Administrator.
  2. Select Sign in.

What to point out

  • Each demo account has a different role and project assignment.
  • What a person can see follows that account, from the first screen onward.

Start a case and upload the notice

PAGA Cases › New PAGA case · Document Upload
Alex MorganPlatform Administrator

A PAGA notice, litigation notice, legal filing or supporting document starts or extends a case. PDFs are read directly in the browser.

What to do

  1. Select New PAGA case.
  2. Drag in a PDF and enter the case details.
  3. Select Create case.

What to point out

  • A new case starts with no alleged counts. They come from its filing, or a reviewer adds them.
  • Text the system extracts is linked to its page and must be confirmed by a person.
  • A document that names another employer, or is not a complaint or notice, is flagged and kept as supporting material unless someone marks it as the filing.
  • Scanned PDFs without a text layer are identified, so details can be entered by hand.
